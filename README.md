# 📚 BookVerse Retail --- Business Intelligence & Sales Analytics

A **MySQL-based Business Intelligence and Sales Analytics project**
focused on analyzing retail sales, customer behaviour, product
performance, and inventory using relational data and SQL.

## 📌 Project Overview

**BookVerse Retail** is a fictional online and offline bookstore with
multiple interconnected business areas --- **Books, Customers, Orders,
Sales, and Inventory**.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate how raw relational data can
be transformed into meaningful business insights using a structured
analytics process:

**Business Understanding → Database Design → Data Validation → SQL
Exploration → Analysis → Insights → Business Recommendations**

The project uses a relational database consisting of three core tables:

-   📚 **Books** --- product, genre, pricing, and stock information
-   👥 **Customers** --- customer details and geographic information
-   🛒 **Orders** --- customer purchases, quantities, dates, and revenue

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis focuses on questions such as:

-   Which books, authors, and genres generate the most revenue?
-   Which books have the highest sales volume?
-   How does sales performance change across months and years?
-   Who are the most active and high-value customers?
-   How does customer purchasing behaviour differ across countries?
-   Which customers purchase books across multiple genres?
-   Which books have never been purchased?
-   Which products have low or high inventory levels?
-   Which books have **high sales but low stock**?
-   Which books have **high stock but low sales**?

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Sales Performance

Analysis of revenue, units sold, books, authors, genres, and
monthly/yearly sales performance.

### 2. Customer Behaviour

Analysis of customer orders, spending, repeat purchases, geographic
behaviour, and multi-genre purchasing.

### 3. Product & Genre Performance

Identification of high-performing products and books with no recorded
sales.

### 4. Inventory Analysis

Identification of low-stock and high-stock books and calculation of
inventory value using:

**Inventory Value = Price × Stock**

### 5. Sales + Inventory Opportunities

Combining sales demand with current stock levels to identify:

-   High Sales + Low Stock → **Replenishment opportunity**
-   High Stock + Low Sales → **Promotion opportunity**
-   Never Ordered + Available Stock → **Catalog / promotional review**
